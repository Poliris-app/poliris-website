## 1. Data We Collect

### 1.1 When You Visit the Website

We may process:

- technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, pages viewed, referring website and similar log data, in particular to ensure the operation, security and protection of the Website;
- data that you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, company name and the content of your message when you use a contact form or request a demonstration.

Data relating to your use of the Website may also be processed by audience measurement tools when they are enabled, under the conditions described in Section 12.

### 1.2 When You Create and Use a Poliris Account

We may process:

- account data: name, business email address, password in hashed form, company and information associated with your account;
- billing information required to manage your subscription and payments;
- Service usage data: actions performed within the interface, configured projects and websites, settings, operation history and exchanges with support;
- content data: domains, URLs, keywords, prompts and web content that you ask Poliris to audit, analyze or process.

Website audits may involve automated crawling of publicly accessible pages of the domains you configure. This crawling operates independently of the WordPress Plugin.

Public content analyzed may occasionally contain personal data. When you ask us to process data concerning third parties, it is your responsibility to ensure that you have an appropriate legal basis for such processing.

### 1.3 When You Use the WordPress Plugin

The Plugin acts as a connector between your WordPress website and your Poliris account.

When pairing a WordPress website, the Plugin may securely transmit to Poliris:

- a WordPress application password associated with the account used for pairing;
- the pairing code;
- the website URL;
- the WordPress username of the administrator performing the pairing;
- the WordPress version;
- the Plugin version;
- the name of the active SEO plugin, where applicable.

Once the website has been paired, Poliris may communicate with it through the WordPress REST API.

When you explicitly approve a change in the Poliris dashboard, Poliris may, in particular, transmit to your website the content required to apply that change, for example:

- a title;
- a meta description;
- page content;
- image alternative text;
- a new article created as a draft.

The Plugin may also perform technical communications necessary to maintain the connection with Poliris, including to retrieve approved changes or report the connection status.

These communications may use a website-specific technical identifier and a timestamp.

### 1.4 When You Use Poli AI

The Service may provide you with a conversational assistant called Poli AI.

When you use Poli AI, we may process:

- the content of your conversations, including your messages, questions, instructions and generated responses;
- contextual information required to provide a response, for example data relating to the project or website concerned by your question;
- technical data, such as the timestamp and session identifier.

Conversations may contain personal data where you choose to provide such information. We recommend that you provide only the information necessary for your request and that you do not provide sensitive or confidential information that is not necessary.

Certain information required to generate a response may be transmitted to our artificial intelligence model providers under the conditions described in Sections 3 and 4.

The Poli AI interface informs users that they are interacting with an artificial intelligence system.

### 1.5 When You Connect Google Analytics or Google Search Console

You may choose to connect a Google Analytics 4 property and/or Google Search Console property to a website configured in Poliris.

This connection is optional and is initiated by you using the authorization mechanism provided by Google.

Poliris requests only the permissions necessary for the features offered, including:

- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly`;
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly`;
- Google account identity information required for the connection, including `openid` and `email`.

These permissions are read-only.

When you connect Google Search Console, Poliris may, in particular, read performance information associated with the selected property, such as:

- queries;
- pages;
- clicks;
- impressions;
- click-through rate;
- average position;
- device;
- country.

When you connect Google Analytics 4, Poliris may, in particular, read certain reporting data relating to the selected property, such as:

- sessions and acquisition channels;
- users;
- aggregated audience characteristics available within your property;
- device type and country;
- engagement metrics;
- aggregated performance data for audited pages.

Poliris does not use these permissions to modify or delete data in your Google Analytics or Google Search Console property.

The specific use of data received from Google APIs is detailed in Section 7.

### 1.6 Business Prospect Data

As part of our commercial activities, we may process professional information relating to individuals who may be interested in Poliris.

This information may include:

- first and last name;
- job title;
- company;
- professional contact details;
- the company’s website;
- relevant professional information used to assess the potential relevance of Poliris.

This data may come from:

- publicly accessible professional sources;
- your company’s website;
- professional networks or directories;
- service providers specializing in identifying professional contacts, such as Apollo;
- our previous interactions with you or your company.

Where data has not been collected directly from you, we will inform you of its use no later than at the time of our first communication with you or within the time limits provided for by applicable law.

Each prospecting communication allows you to easily object to any further solicitation.

### 1.7 What the Plugin Does Not Collect

In its normal operation, the Poliris Plugin is not intended to collect or transmit to Poliris personal data relating to visitors to your own website.

In particular, merely installing the Plugin does not cause it to transmit to Poliris:

- your visitors’ IP addresses;
- their cookies;
- their browsing history;
- their customer or e-commerce data;
- their analytics data.

The Plugin is also not intended to place tracking cookies on your visitors’ browsers.

These statements concern the Poliris Plugin itself and not any other extensions, services or tools that you may use on your WordPress website.

## 5. When Poliris Acts as a Processor

In certain situations, in particular where a business customer asks Poliris to process information contained on its website or within its systems in order to provide the Service, the customer determines the purposes and essential means of the processing.

In this case:

- the customer acts as the data controller;
- POLIRIS acts as the data processor;
- POLIRIS processes the relevant data on the customer’s documented instructions and in order to provide the Service.

Where required, the parties’ respective obligations are governed by a Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”).

The DPA describes, in particular:

- the subject matter and duration of the processing;
- the categories of data and data subjects concerned;
- security measures;
- the conditions governing the use of sub-processors;
- any international transfers;
- the arrangements for returning or deleting data.

Where required by applicable law, such transfers are based on a mechanism recognized under Chapter V of the GDPR, including:

- an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission;
- the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is validly certified and that mechanism applies to the transfer concerned;

## 9. Retention Periods

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

### Accounts and Service Data

Active account data is retained for the duration of the contractual relationship.

After an account is closed, this data is deleted or anonymized within a maximum period of 90 days, unless longer retention is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or defend our rights.

Copies contained in backups may remain for an additional limited period before being purged in accordance with the normal backup cycle.

### WordPress Plugin Data

Authentication information retained by Poliris to access a WordPress website is deleted when the website’s connection to Poliris is removed in accordance with the conditions provided by the Service.

You may also revoke the application password directly from WordPress. Once revoked in WordPress, it can no longer be used by Poliris.

Simply deactivating the Plugin should not be considered a substitute for revoking the application password where you wish to permanently remove authorization.

### Google Integrations

Authorizations retained by Poliris are deleted when you disconnect the corresponding integration.

Reporting data already imported follows the retention period of the project or audit to which it is attached.

### Billing and Accounting

Documents that must be retained to comply with accounting, tax or commercial obligations are retained for the period required by applicable law, which may in particular be up to 10 years.

### Technical and Security Logs

Technical and security logs are retained for a period proportionate to their purpose and, unless there is a specific need, for a maximum period of 12 months.

### Contact Requests

Data relating to a contact request that does not result in a contractual relationship is retained for a maximum period of 24 months from the last meaningful exchange, unless circumstances justify a shorter or longer period.

### Business Prospects

Data used for prospecting purposes relating to an individual who is not a customer is retained for a maximum period of 3 years from its collection or from the prospect’s most recent contact.

Where an individual objects to prospecting, the information strictly necessary to comply with that objection may be retained on a suppression list for an appropriate period, in particular to prevent that individual from being contacted again following a new collection of their contact details.

## 10. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Depending on the systems concerned, these measures include:

- encryption of communications using HTTPS/TLS;
- protection of sensitive credentials and secrets at rest;
- hashing of user passwords;
- access restrictions on production systems;
- application of the principle of least privilege;
- logging of certain technical and security events;
- logical isolation of different customers’ data;
- appropriate backup and restoration mechanisms.

As no computer system can guarantee absolute security, we regularly reassess the measures implemented based on risks and changes to the Service.

### Data Breaches

Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the individuals concerned, we notify the competent supervisory authority in accordance with Article 33 of the GDPR and, where feasible, within 72 hours after becoming aware of it.

Where the conditions of Article 34 of the GDPR are met, we also inform the individuals concerned without undue delay.

Where Poliris acts as a processor, we inform the relevant data controller in accordance with applicable obligations.
